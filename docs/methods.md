# Mathematical methods

For a reduced rational function `f=p/q`, the cross-difference `p(X)q(Y)-p(Y)q(X)` contains the near-separated polynomial associated with any right component. The rational engine factors this polynomial over the declared constants, counts compatible products using both coordinate-block degrees and diagonal vanishing, and reconstructs an inner generator from a nonconstant coefficient. A generic graph Groebner calculation recovers the outer map and tests exact membership in the generated field.

For implicit input `F(x,z)`, the coefficients of a generic monic factor satisfy the equations obtained by division of `F` by that factor. In characteristic zero, the factor algebra of degree `d` has dimension `binomial(deg_x(F),d)`. A separating linear form presents this finite algebra in one coordinate. Factoring its minimal polynomial yields the Galois orbits of factor coefficient tuples, including coefficient extensions that need not lie in a single-root field.

Reconstruction forms the field generated by those coefficients over the declared constants. Supported rational curve models provide a generator for that field and expressions for the coefficients in it. The outer relation is the factor rewritten in that generator; the inner relation is the generator's minimal polynomial over `K(z)`. Exact resultants validate the result, including powers arising from overlapping conjugate factors.

Polynomial parameter reconstruction fixes a monic inner component with zero constant term. Highest coefficients determine the remaining inner coefficients successively; outer coefficients are then recovered in descending order. Residual equations and denominator guards define the exact parameter condition. Degree and residual strata cover exceptional specializations.

Real verification compares the original selected graph with the existentially quantified composition graph. Rational intermediate coordinates can be substituted exactly while preserving their pole guards. An ordered comparison involving `N/D` is cleared by multiplying by `D**2`, with `D != 0` retained. Implicit intermediates use exact quantifier elimination. Totality and uniqueness are separate quantified obligations.

## References

- [Gutiérrez, Rubio and Sevilla, On Multivariate Rational Function Decomposition](https://doi.org/10.1006/jsco.2000.0529), Journal of Symbolic Computation, 2002. The rational engine follows the uni-multivariate decomposition framework.
- [Kozen, Landau and Zippel, Decomposition of Algebraic Functions](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/kozen/Papers/alg.pdf). Function fields, coefficient generators, genus, and resultants motivate the implicit reconstruction. The implemented automatic search has the scope described in the capability matrix.
- [Kozen and Landau, Polynomial Decomposition Algorithms](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~kozen/Papers/poly.pdf). Polynomial structure and symbolic root-finding applications.
